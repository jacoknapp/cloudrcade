Experimental GameCube

Dolphin prototype. Select a local GameCube ISO or GCM to try it. Compatibility and performance are unverified.

Sound and saves are disabled in this prototype. Closing it discards progress.

Requires WebGPU and the device’s GPU. Games over 50 MB stream with a 16 MiB disk cache.

Core is stopped.

Keyboard: X/Z/V/B = A/B/X/Y; Q/E/C = L/R/Z; Enter = Start; arrows = D-pad; WASD = stick; IJKL = C-stick. Standard gamepads are supported.
